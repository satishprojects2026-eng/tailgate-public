Delete Your Tailgate Account & Data

App: Tailgate (com.tailgate.app) · Last updated: May 31, 2026

Tailgate lets you permanently delete your account and associated data at any time. You can do this yourself inside the app, or by emailing us.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tailgate and sign in.
  2. On the Home screen, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and associated data are permanently removed.

Or request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email satish.projects2026@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my Tailgate account". We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login (email address).
  • Your profile data (display name).
  • Your membership in any Tailgate sessions, including any last-shared location.
  • Any pit-stop votes you cast.

What we keep

  • Nothing tied to you after deletion. Location data is only stored while a session is active and is removed when you leave or the session ends; sessions also auto-expire after 12 hours. Once your account is deleted, your associated data is removed.

For more on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
