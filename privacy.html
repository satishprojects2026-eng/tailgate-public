Privacy Policy for Tailgate

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Tailgate ("the app", "we", "us") is a real-time group travel app that lets you and your friends share your live locations on one map while traveling together. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, how it is shared, and how long it is kept.

By using Tailgate you agree to the practices described below.

Who we are

Tailgate is developed and operated by Satish Nagabhyrava.

For any privacy questions or requests, contact: satish.projects2026@gmail.com

What we collect

Account information

  • Email address — used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Display name — the name your friends see on the shared map.

Authentication and account data are handled by our backend provider, Supabase.

Location data

  • Precise (GPS) location — collected while you are in an active Tailgate session so your position can appear on the group's shared map.
  • Background location — if you grant it, the app continues to update your position while the app is in the background during an active session, so your friends can still see you while your phone is in your pocket or mounted for navigation. A persistent notification is shown whenever background location is active. Background location is never collected outside an active session.
  • Speed and travel mode (driving / walking) — derived from your location and shown to your group (e.g., the speed leaderboard and arrival estimates).

Session and device information

  • The destination, host, and membership of sessions you create or join.
  • A basic device platform identifier (e.g., "android") used to support the app across iOS and Android.

We do not collect contacts, photos, microphone, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use your data

  • To show your live location, name, speed, and ETA to other members of a session you have joined.
  • To provide in-app turn-by-turn navigation and routing.
  • To send you in-app notifications (e.g., a buddy has stopped or is drifting from the group, or a pit-stop vote).
  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How your data is shared

  • With other session members. When you join or host a session, your live location, display name, speed, and travel mode are visible in real time to the other people in that same session — that is, anyone you have shared the Tailgate ID / invite link with. Only share session links with people you trust.
  • With service providers we use to run the app:
    • Supabase — database, authentication, and real-time messaging that powers the shared map.
    • Google Maps Platform — map display and the Directions API used for routing and arrival estimates. Your location may be sent to Google to render maps and calculate routes.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

How long we keep your data

  • Location is session-scoped. Your location data exists only while a session is active. When you leave a session, end a session, or it is closed, your member record (including your last shared location) is removed.
  • Sessions auto-expire after 12 hours as a safety net, and are limited to a maximum of 10 members.
  • Account data (email, display name) is retained while your account exists.

Your choices and rights

  • Location permission — you control location access (including background location) in your device settings, and can revoke it at any time. Without location permission the core sharing feature will not work.
  • Notifications — you can disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Delete your account — you can delete your account from within the app, which removes your account and associated data. You can also email satish.projects2026@gmail.com to request deletion.

Children

Tailgate is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections, and the app does not permit unencrypted network traffic. While no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
