Tailgate - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Overview

Tailgate ("we", "our", "the app") is a real-time GPS caravan app that lets friends drive together. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and password (encrypted) when you create an account.
  • Location Data: Your GPS coordinates while you are in an active Tailgate session. This is used to show your car on the map to other members in your group.
  • Session Data: Session IDs, destination addresses, and member information during an active journey.

How We Use Your Data

  • To display your car's location on the map to members in your caravan group.
  • To send you buddy stop alerts and moving away notifications.
  • To authenticate your account.

Data Retention

Location data is temporary. When a Tailgate session ends, all member location data for that session is deleted. We do not store location history, travel logs, or movement patterns.

Data Sharing

Your location is shared only with members of your active Tailgate session. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: We use Supabase for authentication, database, and real-time features. Supabase's privacy policy can be found at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Apple MapKit: We use Apple Maps to display the map and routes. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Permissions

  • Location (Always): Required to track your car on the map, including when the app is in the background or screen is locked.
  • Notifications: Optional. Used for buddy stop alerts and moving away detection.

Your Rights

  • You can stop sharing your location at any time by ending the session or revoking location permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete your account by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Tailgate is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
